ABOUT LITTLE HANDS, BIG STORIES

"Every child carries a world of stories inside them. Little Hands, Big Stories is a creative space where our young writers transform imagination, memories, questions and dreams into stories."

This is the literary home of Secondary First Year — 12th Batch, Noorul Huda Centre for Excellence.

Here, our 38 young authors write stories, diaries, letters, poems, manifestos, book reviews and free writing in English, Malayalam, Manglish, Arabic, Urdu and Kannada — take part in weekly tasks, solve mysteries in the Mystery Room, earn points, stars and badges, and climb the points board.

Every piece is read and approved by the teacher before it is published. 🌟

Big imagination begins with little hands. ✍️📖
